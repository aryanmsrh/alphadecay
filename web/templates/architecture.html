AlphaDecay System Architecture

AlphaDecay analyzes statutory filings, computes financial indicators in Python, flags non-obvious anomalies, and evaluates both sides: the Attacker and the Defender.

Investigation Engine

The Attacker

Assumes the worst. Analyzes calculated anomalies to build a prosecutorial case that management is hiding something behind accounting tricks, channel stuffing, or working capital pressure. Outputs a verdict and evidence strength rating.

The Defender

Assumes the company is acting rationally. Defends using verified operational mechanics, seasonal context, or industry realities. Defines a hard falsification condition where the attacker's claim falls apart, with an evidence strength rating.

Eight-Point Anomaly Schema
1. The Discovery: One clear sentence describing the non-obvious finding (the anomaly).
2. Why It Matters: Three to five bullets explaining the significance of the finding in plain English.
3. Evidence: Multiple independent sources supporting the finding, with links.
4. Timeline: When the change emerged and how it developed.
5. Numbers: Only the calculations and metrics needed to substantiate the finding.
6. Attacker: The attacker's verdict and evidence strength.
7. Defender: The defender's alternative explanation or contradictory evidence along with the falsification condition.
8. Strength: Verdict strength and confidence rating of attacker and defender.
