1. How the System Works (6 Steps)
Math is Calculated in Python, Not by AI

Most financial AI apps mess up basic math because they ask the language model to calculate numbers directly. AI models are notoriously bad at arithmetic. ALPHA-DECAY calculates all ratios and margins in regular Python code first, so the numbers are always 100% accurate before any AI reads them.

STEP 01 // DATA SCRAPING ingestion/

Fetching Company Filings

Downloads multi-year quarterly results, balance sheets, and cash flow statements from Screener.in. Also fetches official earnings call transcripts from the BSE and extracts what management said using PyPDF.

Result: Official numbers & quotes from management.
STEP 02 // CALCULATIONS engine/analytics.py

Financial Calculations Engine

Runs in regular Python code with no AI guessing. Calculates core operating profit, year-on-year sales growth, cash conversion, and working capital days (DSO, DIO, DPO, and Cash Conversion Cycle).

Rule: Matches annual cash flow against annual profit.
STEP 03 // RANKING engine/forensics.py

Spotting & Ranking Divergences

Scans the numbers for warning signs (like cash flow dropping while profits rise, or suppliers waiting longer for payment) and scores each one out of 10 to pick the most interesting finding to test.

Score: Ranked by financial significance.
STEP 04 // TWO-AGENT DEBATE agent/cyclic_pipeline.py

LangGraph Two-Agent Debate

Pits an Investigative Agent against a skeptical Disproving Agent. The first agent proposes a theory based on the numbers and earnings calls. The skeptic tries to shoot it down using ordinary business explanations. If the skeptic disproves it, the agent gets one second try to refine or drop the claim.

Check: Prevents AI from just confirming its own guesses.
STEP 05 // REVIEW & VERDICT agent/nodes.py

Final Review & Verdict

A separate reviewer node decides who made the stronger case. It issues a clear pass or fail verdict: HYPOTHESIS PASSED (if the finding held up under scrutiny) or HYPOTHESIS FAILED (if normal business context explained it away), and states what future data would disprove it.

Rule: Strict pass or fail judgment.
STEP 06 // REPORTS exporter/ & webapp/

Reports and Web Dashboard

Generates a clean 2-page dark PDF research report, draws a dual-axis sales vs. margin chart, and saves the full result to the local run store.

Outputs: 2-Page PDF · Chart · JSON file
2. How the Two Agents Debate (LangGraph Workflow)
Fact-Checking Workflow

If you ask a regular AI to find an issue with a company, it will almost always agree with you and make mountains out of molehills (confirmation bias). ALPHA-DECAY solves this by having two agents argue from opposite perspectives before reaching a conclusion:

01
Investigative Agent (The Prosecution)

Looks at the top financial divergence and checks it against quotes from earnings call transcripts. It writes Attempt 1: linking the numbers (like cash flow dropping) to business decisions (like pushing inventory to stores or paying suppliers early).

02
Disproving Agent (The Defense)

Looks at the exact same data, but its only job is to challenge and disprove the first agent's theory. It checks if there is a completely normal business reason for the shift: seasonal buying before monsoons, raw material price shifts, or strong supplier leverage.

03
Second Attempt (Attempt 1 vs Attempt 2)

If the defense disproves Attempt 1, the pipeline doesn't give up immediately. The Investigative Agent reads the defense's points, fixes the weak spots in its argument, and tries Attempt 2. If the skeptic still disproves it, the finding is thrown out and marked as refuted.

04
Reviewer Node (The Judge)

Reads both sides and makes the final call. It outputs a binary result: HYPOTHESIS PASSED (the finding survived the skeptic's questions) or HYPOTHESIS FAILED (the skeptic was right, it's just normal business), and drafts the 8-point brief.

4. Financial & Business Terms for Beginners
Plain English Guide

New to finance or business reports? Here is an everyday explanation for all the terms and concepts used across this website:

Category A: The Difference Between "Cash" and "Accounting Profit"

EBITDA Operating Profit
What it means: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. It's the profit a company makes directly from running its factories and selling goods, before paying taxes or accounting for aging machinery.
CFO (Cash Flow from Operations) Real Cash
What it means: The actual cash collected from customers minus actual cash paid out to suppliers and staff. Unlike EBITDA (which counts unpaid customer bills as revenue), CFO only counts actual money that entered the bank.
Cash vs Profit Decoupling Warning Sign
What it means: When profit on paper goes up, but bank balance cash goes down. Normally, at least 80% of profit turns into cash. If it drops to 70%, money is stuck in unpaid invoices or unsold inventory.

Category B: Working Capital & The Supply Chain Trio

Debtor Days (DSO)

Payment collection speed: How many days distributors take to pay for products. If this jumps from 18 to 28 days, distributors are taking longer to pay or too much unsold stock was pushed onto store shelves.

Inventory Days (DIO)

Warehouse speed: How many days finished products (like soap or tea) sit in company warehouses before getting sold. A sudden rise means consumer demand is cooling down.

Payable Days (DPO)

Paying suppliers: How many days the company takes to pay raw material vendors (palm oil, packaging). High days (120+) show strong bargaining power over suppliers.

Negative Working Capital (The FMCG Secret)

In most businesses, you have to spend cash up front to build inventory and wait for customers to pay you. But FMCG giants like Hindustan Unilever operate on a negative Cash Conversion Cycle (-89 days). They collect cash from stores in 19 days, but don't pay suppliers for 138 days. Suppliers are essentially funding their daily operations interest-free!

Category C: Margins, Basis Points & Company Filings

Basis Points (bps) vs Percentage Points (pp)

In finance, 1% equals 100 basis points. If a profit margin goes from 23.2% to 24.6%, it went up by +1.4 percentage points or +140 basis points (bps). It’s just an easy way to talk about small percentage changes without getting confused.

Volume Growth vs Price Growth

If revenue grew +4% but prices were hiked +6%, the company actually sold -2% fewer physical packs (negative volume). Companies can mask falling consumer demand by simply raising prices during inflation.

SEBI LODR Regulation 33

The official Indian law requiring all listed public companies to publish audited quarterly financial statements within 45 days of the quarter ending. This is the official data source ALPHA-DECAY scrapes.

Falsification Condition

A proper scientific test should state what evidence would prove it wrong. In every ALPHA-DECAY report, the agent specifies what future result (e.g. cash flow conversion recovering above 82%) would disprove its conclusion.

5. Real Example: Hindustan Unilever (HINDUNILVR)
Tested in Live Trials

The Discovered Signal:

"Hindustan Unilever's operating profit grew, but its Cash Flow from Operations dropped by -8.9% because the company paid its suppliers faster rather than distributors struggling to pay."

1. What the Numbers Showed
  • Cash Conversion: Fell from 80.9% to 73.1% YoY (below normal 80% baseline).
  • Trade Payables: Supplier payment days dropped by 12 days as terms shortened.
  • Debtor Velocity: Improved from 23 to 19 days, showing distributors were healthy.
2. How the Agents Tested It

The Disproving Agent pushed back, pointing out that HUL’s negative cash conversion cycle (-89 days) gave it plenty of cash buffer. The Investigative Agent updated Attempt 2 to show that cash was deployed intentionally to suppliers to lock in early payment discounts.
