1. End-to-End System Pipeline Architecture
6 Discrete Phases · Zero LLM Math Hallucination

Most financial AI applications fail because they treat an LLM as an all-in-one calculator, search engine, and synthesizer. Large language models inherently hallucinate arithmetic when performing complex ratios. ALPHA-DECAY decouples the arithmetic engine entirely from language reasoning, ensuring 100% mathematical determinism.

Statutory Data Ingestion

Harvests multi-year quarterly P&L statements, annual balance sheets, and audited cash flows from official SEBI LODR filings via Screener.in. Downloads official BSE India earnings call PDFs and parses dialogue blocks using PyPDF.

Output: Audited statements & verbatim management quotes.
PHASE 02 // DETERMINISM engine/analytics.py

Forensic Math Engine

Executes in pure Python with zero LLM inference. Computes Core EBITDA (ex-other income), YoY revenue growth, CFO conversion, Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), Days Inventory (DIO), Days Payable (DPO), and Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC).

Safeguard: Strict annual-to-annual period alignment.
PHASE 03 // TRIAGE engine/forensics.py

Anomaly Ranking & Scoring

Applies quantitative heuristics across financial ratios. Detects and ranks divergences out of 10.0 (CFO conversion decoupling, working capital stretches, margin wedges) to select the top candidate anomaly for adversarial testing.

Scoring: Divergences ranked by capital severity.
PHASE 04 // ADVERSARIAL agent/cyclic_pipeline.py

LangGraph Multi-Agent Loop

Pits the Investigative Agent (Prosecutor) against the Disproving Agent (Defense). Uses a 2-attempt self-correcting cycle. If the defense breaks down the initial thesis with benign commercial context, Attempt 2 is refined.

Epistemic Boundary: Eliminates confirmation bias.
PHASE 05 // VERDICT agent/nodes.py

Binary Adjudication Gate

An independent judge node evaluates whether the prosecution withstood defense refutations. Emits strictly binary terminal verdicts: HYPOTHESIS PASSED or HYPOTHESIS FAILED, framing falsification criteria.

Methodology: Karl Popper's empirical falsification.

Artifacts: report.pdf · chart.png · run.json
2. The LangGraph Adversarial Cyclic StateGraph
Epistemic Boundary Control

In single-prompt LLM agents, when an agent is asked to "find something wrong", it experiences confirmation bias: it invents or exaggerates minor accounting noise to please the user. ALPHA-DECAY solves this using a two-sided adversarial debate structured as a LangGraph state machine:

01
Investigative Agent (The Prosecution)

Ingests the top-ranked financial divergence and cross-examines it against management quotes from BSE earnings calls. It formulates Attempt 1 Hypothesis: connecting numbers (e.g. CFO drop) to operational tactics (e.g. aggressive channel distribution or supplier credit contractions).

02
Disproving Agent (The Defense)

Given the exact same filings, the defense's sole mandate is to falsify and dismantle the hypothesis. It tests whether benign commercial realities explain the shift: negative cash conversion cycles (-89 days for HUL) proving supplier funding, pre-monsoon buffer inventories, or commodity price fluctuations.

03
Cyclic Refinement Gate (Attempt 1 vs Attempt 2)

If the defense refutes Attempt 1, the pipeline does not stop. The Investigative Agent ingests the defense’s critique, isolates where the initial logic was weak, and formulates Attempt 2 to test if a narrower, undeniable finding holds. If the finding is still broken down, it is abandoned and marked as refuted.

04
Independent Adjudication Gate (The Judge)

Acts as the terminal verification gate. It compares the prosecution's evidence against the defense's refutation. It issues a binary verdict (HYPOTHESIS PASSED if it survived adversarial challenge, or HYPOTHESIS FAILED if benign factors explained it), frames explicit falsification conditions, and drafts the 8-point brief.

4. Financial & Business Forensic Glossary for Beginners
Plain English Masterclass

New to corporate finance and business analysis? Here is everything you need to know about the terms, concepts, and forensic checks used throughout ALPHA-DECAY:

Category A: The Difference Between "Cash" and "Accounting Profit"

EBITDA Profitability
What it means: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. It measures pure operating profit from core factory operations before accounting adjustments like taxes or how fast machinery depreciates.
CFO (Cash Flow from Operations) Liquid Cash
What it means: The actual cash collected from customers minus actual cash paid out to workers and raw material suppliers. Unlike EBITDA (which counts unpaid bills as revenue), CFO only counts hard money in the bank.
CFO-to-EBITDA Decoupling Forensic Anomaly
What it means: When a company's accounting EBITDA rises on paper, but its actual bank cash flow (CFO) shrinks. In healthy companies, CFO conversion is >80%. When it drops to 70%, money is being tied up in unpaid invoices or unsold inventory.

Category B: Working Capital & The Supply Chain Trio

Debtor Days (DSO)

The Collection Speed: How many days distributors take to pay for goods shipped. If DSO increases from 18 to 28 days, it means wholesale distributors are struggling or the company is extending easy credit to artificially boost sales.

Inventory Days (DIO)

Warehouse Velocity: How many days shampoo, soap, or biscuits sit in company depots before being sold. A sudden spike indicates consumer retail demand has slowed down.

Payable Days (DPO)

Supplier Credit Terms: How many days the company takes to pay raw material vendors (e.g. palm oil, chemicals, packaging). A high DPO (120+ days) shows high market clout over suppliers.

Negative Cash Conversion Cycle (The FMCG Secret)

In normal industries, companies must invest cash to build inventory and wait for customers to pay (positive cycle). But elite FMCG firms like Hindustan Unilever operate with a negative Cash Conversion Cycle (-89 days). They collect cash from distributors in ~19 days, but wait ~138 days to pay suppliers. Suppliers are literally funding the company's daily operations interest-free!

Category C: Margins, Basis Points & Statutory Filings

Basis Points (bps) vs Percentage Points (pp)

In finance, 1% equals 100 basis points. If operating profit margin expands from 23.2% to 24.6%, it increased by +1.4 percentage points or +140 basis points (bps). Traders use basis points to avoid confusion when discussing changes in percentages.

Volume Growth vs Realization Growth

If revenue grew +4% but prices were hiked +6%, the company actually sold -2% fewer physical bottles or packages (negative volume). Pure revenue growth can hide dying consumer demand during inflationary periods.

SEBI LODR Regulation 33

The statutory law enacted by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) mandating that every listed public company must submit audited quarterly financial results within 45 days of the quarter-end. This is the primary legally binding data source parsed by ALPHA-DECAY.

Karl Popper's Falsification Condition

In science, an assertion is only valid if you can state what empirical evidence would prove you wrong. In every ALPHA-DECAY brief, the agent must define a Falsification Condition: an explicit future audited disclosure (e.g. CFO recovering >82%) that would refute its conclusion.
